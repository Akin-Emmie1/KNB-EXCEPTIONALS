Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Business privacy information should be reviewed before launch.

Information we collect

Appointment requests collect the name and contact details you provide, plus the selected service and requested date and time. The business uses these details to manage and respond to appointment requests.

How we use information

Appointment records are stored in the business database so administrators can review and manage bookings. Product enquiries open in WhatsApp and are then handled under WhatsApp's terms.

WhatsApp and payments

There is no payment processing on this site. WhatsApp messages are sent only after you choose to open WhatsApp.

Storage and sharing

Product images are served as static files from the website. Business privacy contact details and any additional data-handling disclosures must be configured and reviewed before launch.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you using the business contact details configured on this site.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent using the business contact details configured on this site.

Delivery
